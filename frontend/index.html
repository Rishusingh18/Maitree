Help Desk

Standard Operating Procedures (SOP) & Emergency Contacts.

SOP-01

Yellow Line Platform Dwell

When passengers dwell beyond the tactile warning line for > 3 seconds, automated PA audio plays. If passenger remains after 10 seconds, station staff are alerted to clear the edge. The AI vision system monitors the yellow line boundary using calibrated platform geometry overlays.

SOP-02

Crowd Density Surge Response

When platform density exceeds 75% capacity, the system triggers automated crowd management announcements directing passengers to less congested areas. If density exceeds 90%, OCC Controller activates skip-stop protocols and coordinates with station marshals.

SOP-03

Unattended Baggage Protocol

Object detected stationary on platform for > 120 seconds triggers an automated alert. Station staff verify ownership via PA announcement. If unclaimed after 5 minutes, CISF bomb disposal squad is notified and a 50-metre exclusion zone is established around the object.

SOP-04

Track Intrusion Protocol

Human or foreign object in track envelope triggers automated RED signals on approaching trains. Controller verifies video, dispatches CISF QRT, and authorizes emergency traction power cutoff (750V DC third rail de-energization) if required. The track zone is defined per RDSO safety specification.
